Our Process

The Sheltertech Design & Architecture Workflow

1

Client & Vision Alignment

We discuss your lifestyle, functional needs, budget parameters, and aesthetic preferences.

2

Schematic Design

Initial ideas are translated into preliminary spatial layouts, mood boards, and architectural sketches exploring form and flow.

3

Design Development & Documentation

Approved concepts evolve into precise floor plans, interior architecture packages, and technical specifications.

4

Project Realisation & Implementation

Coordination & Build: We oversee construction progress, coordinate trades, and manage quality control on site.

5

Installation & Styling

Final furniture placement, interior styling, and accessories complete the space prior to handover.
